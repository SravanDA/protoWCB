The Mission

Robots for What Humans Shouldn't Do.

Suspended rope access and exterior cradles remain among the most dangerous operations in property maintenance. ELMO eliminates fall hazard entirely—anchoring securely to vertical glass, traversing complex architectural mullions, and cleaning autonomously with continuous ground telemetry.

Autonomous Boundary Tracking

Multi-sensor optical array scans window joints, structural silicone seams, and architectural edges in real time.

Adhesion Safety

Dual-Circuit Vacuum System

Engineering Architecture

Field-Tested Industrial Architecture

Engineered for vertical stability, continuous multi-story traversal, and fail-safe operation on modern commercial glass.

Dual Vacuum Redundancy

Primary and auxiliary pneumatic pumps maintain continuous holding force with instantaneous fail-over lock.

All-Weather Traversal

High-torque drive tracks deliver controlled vertical traverse in gust conditions up to 40 km/h with active drift compensation.

Mullion & Edge Tracking

Multi-spectral optical array maps structural silicone seams and architectural mullions without contacting framing.

12 kg Monocoque Frame

Lightweight structural composite frame transported and deployed by a single technician without mechanical lifting rigs.

Live Ground Telemetry

Continuous RF uplink streams suction pressure margin, battery cell state, and cleaning telemetry to ground operators.

Zero Roof Rigging

Operates independently of heavy building maintenance units (BMUs), permanent roof tracks, or street crane permits.

Standard Operating Procedure

Deployment Workflow

01

Transit & Setup

Unit arrives in a single transit flight case. Ground station positioned and safety perimeter established in under 15 minutes.

02

Surface Adhesion

ELMO mounts to the glass. Dual vacuum circuits engage with automated barometric diagnostic verifying 200 kg holding force.

03

Autonomous Mapping

Optical sensors identify window boundaries, mullion joints, and structural returns to calculate the optimal cleaning path.

04

Continuous Execution

Cleans 4,000 sq ft per hour with micro-mist scrubbing and live telemetry, returning to docking position for rapid recovery.
